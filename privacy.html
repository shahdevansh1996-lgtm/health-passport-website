Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 18, 2026

Health Passport values your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect information when you use the Health Passport mobile app and website.

Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Account information such as your email address and login credentials.
  • Health-related information you choose to store in the app, including conditions, medicines, documents, and vaccination records.
  • Basic technical information needed to operate and secure the service.

How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account.
  • To securely store and display your personal health information.
  • To support app features such as medication reminders and record management.
  • To maintain the security and reliability of the service.

Data Storage and Security

Your information is stored using cloud-based services designed to support secure authentication and data storage. We take reasonable steps to protect your information from unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or disclosure.

Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. We only share information when required to operate the service, comply with legal obligations, or protect the rights and safety of users and the platform.

Your Choices

  • You can review and update your information inside the app.
  • You can stop using the service at any time.
  • You may contact us with requests related to your account or data.

Children’s Privacy

Health Passport is not intended for unsupervised use by children. If family information is managed in the app, it should be done by a parent, guardian, or authorized adult.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updated versions will be posted on this page.

Contact

For privacy-related questions, you can contact Health Passport support through the contact details provided with the app or business website.
