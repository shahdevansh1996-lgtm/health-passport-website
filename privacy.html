Last updated: April 2026

We may collect and store the following types of information:

Your data is securely stored using trusted cloud services (such as Firebase by Google). We use authentication and encryption measures to protect your information from unauthorized access.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal or health information. Your data is not shared with third parties except when required to provide core services or comply with legal obligations.

5. Sensitive Data

Health Passport handles sensitive personal health information. This data is only stored and accessed for your personal use and is protected using secure systems.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Your data is stored as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your data by contacting us.

7. Your Rights

  • You can access and update your data within the app
  • You can stop using the service at any time
  • You may request account or data deletion

8. Children’s Privacy

This app is not intended for unsupervised use by children. Any family-related data should be managed by a responsible adult.
