Delete Your Account

If you wish to delete your Health Passport account and associated personal data, please follow the steps below.

How to Request Account Deletion

  1. Email us at: healthpassport2026@gmail.com
  2. Use subject: "Delete Account Request"
  3. Include your registered email ID in the message

What Happens After Request

Once we receive your request, we will verify your identity and process the deletion within 7 days.

Data Deletion Details

  • Your account will be permanently deleted
  • All personal data including health records will be deleted
  • No data will be retained after deletion

Contact

If you have any questions, contact us at: healthpassport2026@gmail.com
